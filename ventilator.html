    Manual specifications (reference): tidal volume 0.010 to 4.8 L; FiO₂ 21 to 91 %; pressure relief 0 to 100 cmH₂O; sigh 150 % of VT; supply 3.5 bar (2 to 6 bar); ambient-air valve opens below −10 cmH₂O; high-pressure valve above 110 cmH₂O; high-frequency ventilation 5 to 20 Hz. This simulator uses narrower ranges for teaching.

    Model: inspiration with constant flow gives Paw = PEEP + V/C + R × flow. Expiration is passive: V decays with time constant R × C. Dead space assumed 150 mL. If expiration is too short, volume stays in the lung (air trapping / auto-PEEP).
